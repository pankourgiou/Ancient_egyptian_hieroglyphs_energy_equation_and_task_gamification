Each round starts with a random hieroglyph (new ones are favored until you have collected them all) in place of the number 1, and you can swap it for any other with the glyph buttons (this restarts the round with new numbers), so E = (glyph) × mc² means E = m × c². Finish both tasks to collect the glyph. If you see empty boxes instead of hieroglyphs, your device needs an Egyptian Hieroglyphs font (it loads online automatically).
